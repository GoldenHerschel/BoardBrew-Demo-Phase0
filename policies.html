Privacy notice

This notice explains how (“BoardBrew”, “we”) handles personal information collected through this website, as required by the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Who to contact

Privacy contact:

What we collect and why

When you request a reservation, we ask for your name, mobile number, the date, time and group size, and any notes you choose to add. We use these only to confirm and manage your booking and to contact you about it.

We don’t ask you to create an account. This website doesn’t use cookies, ads or analytics, and doesn’t track you.

Please don’t put health or other sensitive information in the notes.

How the booking form works

The website doesn’t send or store what you type. It prepares a message on your own device, and you choose whether to send it to us on Messenger. Once you send it, we receive it in our Messenger inbox, and Meta’s own terms and privacy policy also apply to that conversation.

When you submit the form, your details are sent over a secure connection to our booking system and saved there so our staff can confirm your table.

Who sees it

Only BoardBrew staff who handle reservations. We don’t sell your information or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We delete booking details days after the booking date, unless we need them longer to resolve a problem with your visit.

Other services

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your device’s IP address. Links to Messenger, foodpanda, GrabFood, our online store and our social media pages take you to other companies’ sites. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to be informed about how your data is used, and to access, correct, object to the use of, and ask us to delete or block your personal data. You also have the right to data portability, to claim damages, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above.

How we protect it

Only staff who need booking details can see them, and we delete them once they’re no longer needed.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this notice and the date at the top of this page.
