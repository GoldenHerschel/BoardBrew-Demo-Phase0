How a BoardBrew visit works

Get a table

Walk in or book ahead online. Bigger groups should reserve, especially on weekends.

Pick a game

Choose from the shelf. Not sure what to play? Ask the staff and they'll teach you the rules.

Order and play

Coffee, drinks and snacks come to your table. Stay for one round or ten.
